How stocks get into Minotaur, how they are ranked and kept current, and what each figure means.

1 · Seed universe

Start from lists that professionals already screen for size and liquidity, rather than the whole exchange:

  • Gold: VanEck Junior Gold Miners ETF (GDXJ) holdings
  • Silver: Amplify Junior Silver Miners ETF (SILJ) holdings
  • Copper: Sprott Junior Copper Miners ETF (COPJ) holdings
  • Rare earths: VanEck Rare Earth & Strategic Metals ETF (REMX) holdings
  • Plus mining names in the TSX Venture 50 and the S&P/ASX Small Ordinaries, to catch smaller juniors

4 · Balance the list

  • By metal: about 35 gold, 30 copper, 15 silver, 20 rare earths
  • By stage: about 40 explorers (pre-study), 40 developers (PEA to construction), 20 early producers
  • By country: no more than ~25% in any one country
  • Always in: your holdings and watchlist, whatever their score
  • Bench: the next 20 by score, ready to swap in

5 · Keeping it current

  • Data refresh: prices, news, studies, funding, targets and milestones by a scheduled Claude research run twice a week (Wednesdays refresh half the list, Saturdays the other half). The invisory.uk version loads the latest published snapshot from GitHub each time it opens.
  • Re-rank monthly; rotate the list quarterly
  • Drop on completed takeover, delisting, or falling below the hard filters
  • Every figure must come from a fetched source, cross-checked where possible; unverifiable figures are left unchanged and noted

6 · Definitions

Stage bar1 Exploration · 2 Resource · 3 Scoping/PEA (Preliminary Economic Assessment) · 4 PFS (Pre-Feasibility Study) · 5 DFS/FS (Definitive Feasibility Study) · 6 Permitting & finance · 7 Construction · 8 Production. Shows the most advanced asset.
NPVPost-tax net present value of the flagship project from the latest published study, 100% basis, in the study's currency and price deck
P/NPVMarket cap ÷ study NPV, both converted to US$. Ignores ownership share, net cash/debt and study-date prices
NPV-implied priceStudy NPV × 0.3 / 0.5 / 1.0 ÷ shares on issue. Developers typically trade at 0.3–0.5× before funding, nearer 1× in production
Broker targetsNamed broker, dated, published target only; flagged where the broker also managed a capital raise
MilestonesCompany-guided timing; “Slipped” means guidance has moved later
Map pinsApproximate project centroids, not drill collars
